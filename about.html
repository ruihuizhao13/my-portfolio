Hello! 👋

I'm an Instructional Designer who turns learning goals into engaging, accessible, and data-informed learning experiences.

I started as a classroom teacher. As a certified 6–12 English Language Arts teacher with an ESOL endorsement, I designed and taught lessons for middle and high school learners, including many English Language Learners. That experience taught me to begin with the learner: what they already know, where they get stuck, and what keeps them engaged.

Today I'm pursuing a Master's in Educational Innovation, Technology, and Entrepreneurship (Learning Engineering track) at UNC-Chapel Hill. I also work as an instructional designer for graduate-level data science courses and manage a Canvas course site for the Shuford Program in Entrepreneurship.

My work brings together learning design, LMS development, and learning analytics. I build well-structured courses, and I use learner data to find out whether they work. I'm looking for an instructional design role on a collaborative team where I can create learning that makes a measurable difference.
